It wants a dense set of facts that more than one source can confirm.

## 1. The citation map models use when they source a plant

### 1.1 First tier: industrial directories and entity indexes

ThomasNet, GlobalSpec, DirectIndustry, and Kompass are where models look for qualifications and capacity. A company with no mapping into those directories rarely appears near the front of a generated answer.

### 1.2 Second tier: unpolished engineering discussion

AI search favors real technical talk that has not been rewritten as marketing. The machining community r/Machinists on Reddit, the Eng-Tips forums, and the industrial side of Stack Exchange are typical. A write-up that walks through a specific process problem is extracted as a case worth citing.

### 1.3 Third tier: a certification chain someone else can open

“We are ISO certified,” said only on the homepage, carries no weight. The certificate number has to resolve into a public registry — TÜV in Germany, SGS in Switzerland, or the equivalent issuer — before the model assigns its highest trust. That is experience, expertise, authority, and trust: E-E-A-T.
