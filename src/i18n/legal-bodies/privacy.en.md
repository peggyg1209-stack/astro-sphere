## 1. What we collect

To answer a booking request and prepare a focused first diagnosis, we collect only the minimum information you choose to give us through the contact form:

**1.1 Contact details.** Your name, work email, and phone or messaging account.

**1.2 Company and business context.** Company name, industry segment, and target markets for going global (for example North America, Europe, Japan, or Southeast Asia).

**1.3 Needs and technical clues.** The brief you submit, the URL of a site you want diagnosed, or an outline of technical parameters.

**1.4** The site does not require an account, and we do not ask for payment-card numbers or other sensitive financial credentials.

## 2. Why we use it

We use this information only to:

**2.1** Provide the agreed 15-minute overseas AI trust-gap scan and a short technical briefing.

**2.2** Arrange a conversation and a time with a dedicated advisor for the topic you selected (export infrastructure, industry–academia field study, cross-boundary study travel, think-tank co-creation, and similar).

**2.3** Assess whether an in-person private field visit or an advanced study program is a fit.

## 3. Confidentiality and security

**3.1 Strict confidence.** Process parameters, unpublished part numbers, and site-architecture details you share in a technical review or briefing are treated as trade secrets. We do not publish them without permission.

**3.2 No sale of data.** We do not sell, rent, or unlawfully share your personal or company data with commercial data brokers.

**3.3 Limited operational sharing.** Only when it is necessary to deliver a specific technical implementation (for example an overseas compliance-monitoring tool) or to host a study visit abroad, we share the minimum required information with a technical partner or an on-the-ground host who is bound by confidentiality.

## 4. Cookies and technical logs

**4.1** We use only lightweight cookies or analytics needed to run the site, protect it, and understand basic traffic.

**4.2** We do not use tracking scripts to build cross-site profiles or to serve intrusive advertising.
